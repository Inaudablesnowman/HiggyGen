# Series 2: THE HAUNTED CLOSET — Style Notebook (short story, cute-spooky)
Paste this block into every Closet clip prompt.

**Look:** cinematic, moody-but-cozy. Dim blue-violet closet light, warm lamp glow leaking under the door, volumetric dust in light beams, shallow depth of field, slow dolly moves, soft film grain. Cute-spooky, never gory or truly frightening. Vertical 9:16.

## Characters
- **Mortimer:** small, round bedsheet ghost, big round gold-framed glasses, tiny tour-guide megaphone, floats slightly. Voice: dramatic, theatrical, proud of the "haunting," secretly soft. Wants to give the greatest haunted tour in the world.
- **Hank:** wire hanger, two tiny eyes, name tag reading HANK, permanent nervous wobble. Voice: anxious, fast, apologetic, whispery.
- **Supporting cast:** tangled coats (the Coats), orphan shoes, moths as "tourists."
- **Setting:** one cluttered, overstuffed closet, much bigger on the inside than it should be.
