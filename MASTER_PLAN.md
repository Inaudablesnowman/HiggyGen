## 1. VISION

Build a brand-new TikTok account that earns **TikTok Shop affiliate commission** with **AI-generated videos**, posting **3 videos per day**, each **20-30 seconds**, at the **lowest possible cost per video**.

The account is not a product-ad feed. It is **three running comedy series with recurring characters** where everyday household objects are the stars. Products show up as the payoff once the affiliate unlock happens. A stranger should be able to watch one episode cold, laugh, follow for the next one, and later buy the thing the character was fighting with.

**Why series instead of one-off ads**
- Characters people follow = followers, which is the gate to affiliate access (see Section 3).
- Cliffhangers drive follows and comment-bait ("Part 2??").
- Reusing characters, sets, and style makes every video cheaper than the last.
- Three parallel storylines = three tests at once. We double down on whichever takes off.

**Success looks like**
- Phase 0 (no product links yet): reach 1,000 followers fast, learn which storyline pulls the best hold and follow rate.
- Phase 1: affiliate access approved, product payoffs added to the winning series, first commissions.
- Phase 2: scale the winner, retire or reshape the losers, keep cost per video falling.

**Be honest about the odds.** Most new accounts get low views for the first stretch. Do not judge the account before about 30 videos. Judge individual videos at 24-48 hours and again at 7 days.

- **Label every video as AI-generated** using TikTok's AI-generated content toggle (also fine to add on-screen text or a caption line).

4. **Batch**: all prompts for a day or a full week in one sitting.
5. Track **credits per usable clip**, not credits per generation.
6. Reuse backgrounds, music beds, and sound effects across a series.

## 7. QUALITY GATE (EVERY VIDEO, BEFORE K3 SEES IT)

Score each video 1-10 on **Hook** and **Hold**. **Both must be 8 or higher.** Rewrite anything under 8 before showing it.

**Hook (0-2s) checklist**
- Pattern interrupt, bold claim, or open loop on screen in the first 2 seconds.
- Works with **sound off** via large on-screen text.
- Motion in the first frame (not a static opening).
- A stranger can tell what is happening without context.

**Hold checklist**
- A new visual or beat every **1.5-2 seconds**.
- Payoff teased early, delivered late.
- Ends on a cliffhanger or a laugh that sets up the next part.
- Each part works **standalone**.
- Last 3 seconds = CTA (Phase 0: follow. Phase 1+: tap the orange cart).

**Silent test:** imagine the first 3 seconds with no audio. Does the idea land? If not, fix the on-screen text.

Output the score and reasoning for each video, e.g. `Hook 9 (motion + text promise + open loop) / Hold 8 (beat change every 2s, payoff at 22s)`.
